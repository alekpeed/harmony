# Curriculum content

Gate definitions land here in Phase 6, authored against the checklist in
`docs/spec/21_CONTENT_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md` §3 and validated by `./gradlew validateHarmonyContent`.

Kept separate from `exercises/` on purpose: a gate answers "what competency is being tested and
how is mastery decided", an exercise policy answers "what material may be generated". Merging
the two into one JSON object is the thing the authoring guide §2 warns against.
